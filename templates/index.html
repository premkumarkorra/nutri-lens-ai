Engine Operating Guidelines

  • Real-World Product Identification: Identifies specific commercial products and brands based on ingredient signatures.
  • Finished Product Alternatives Only: Strictly recommends recognizable finished food products (e.g. Lay's Classic, Oreo Original), never raw ingredients (e.g. ❌ "use rice").
  • Anti-Hallucination Protection: If ingredients are insufficient or generic, marks as Insufficient rather than fabricating a brand.
  • Match Confidence Categories: Clearly distinguishes Exact, Strong, Likely, Possible, and Insufficient matches.
